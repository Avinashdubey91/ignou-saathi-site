IGNOU Saathi

A personal study helper for IGNOU students on Windows. Unofficial. Not affiliated with IGNOU.

IGNOU Saathi keeps a student's own IGNOU information in one place on their own PC: courses, assignments and their due dates, exam dates and hall tickets, marks, cover pages and remittance forms, and the receipts of exam forms and re-registration. It reads IGNOU's public pages and, after the student signs in to IGNOU Samarth themselves, the student's own Samarth pages.

Google Drive

If the student chooses, the app can copy the files it saves into the student's own Google Drive, in the same folders as on the PC. The app asks Google only for permission to the files it creates itself (the "drive.file" permission) and for the student's email address, to show which account is connected. It cannot see or change any other file in the student's Drive. The student can disconnect at any time in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
