Privacy Policy

IGNOU Saathi (Windows app). Unofficial. Not affiliated with IGNOU. Last updated: 23 October 2026.

What the app keeps

The app keeps the student's IGNOU details (enrolment number, name, address, programme, courses, marks, dates) and the files it saves (assignments, forms, receipts, hall tickets, question papers) only on the student's own PC. There is no server of IGNOU Saathi: the app sends nothing to its makers, has no analytics and no crash upload.

Where the app connects

  • IGNOU's own websites, to read the student's information and IGNOU's public pages.
  • IGNOU Samarth, only after the student signs in there themselves; the app never types a CAPTCHA or presses a submit button.
  • Google Drive, only if the student connects it (below).

Google Drive (optional)

When the student presses "Connect Google Drive", Google asks the student to allow two things: access to only the files this app creates (Google's "drive.file" permission) and the student's email address (to show which account is connected). The app then copies the files of the folders the student ticks into a folder the student chooses in their own Drive. Study material is never copied. The app does not read, change or delete any other file in the student's Drive, and it never deletes files in Drive. The sign-in is kept on the student's PC in Windows' protected storage. The student can disconnect in the app, which forgets the sign-in, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the copy to the student's own Drive; it is not shared, sold, or used for advertising, and no person reads it.

Contact

Questions: write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for IGNOU Saathi.
